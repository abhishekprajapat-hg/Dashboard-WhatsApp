IT And Technology Services

The following is an India-focused industry audit covering software development, SaaS, artificial intelligence and machine learning, cloud computing, cybersecurity, IT consulting, managed services, system integration, data analytics and IT-enabled business services. Your source document classifies IT & Technology Services under the tertiary sector and includes software development, SaaS, AI and machine learning, cloud computing, cybersecurity and IT consulting.
1. End-to-End Business Flow
The industry operates through several related business models:
Custom software development: Lead generation → requirement discovery → proposal → development → testing → deployment → support and enhancement
SaaS: Market research → product development → customer acquisition → subscription onboarding → usage adoption → renewal and expansion
IT consulting: Client problem identification → assessment → solution roadmap → implementation support → transformation management
Cloud and managed services: Infrastructure assessment → migration → configuration → monitoring → optimisation → recurring managed support
Cybersecurity services: Risk assessment → security design → implementation → monitoring → incident response → compliance improvement
AI and data services: Data discovery → use-case selection → model development → deployment → monitoring → continuous improvement
Sequential process map

5. Future Growth Prospects
Revenue trends
India’s IT and IT-enabled services industry remains one of the country’s largest export and employment sectors. MeitY states that the IT-BPM industry, excluding e-commerce, is expected to reach approximately US$315 billion in FY2025–26, including exports of about US$246 billion. Employment is estimated at roughly six million professionals, with about 135,000 jobs added over the previous year. (MeitY)
The next three to five years are likely to be shaped less by traditional labour-based outsourcing alone and more by:
Artificial intelligence and automation

SaaS and industry-specific software

2. IndiaAI infrastructure
The IndiaAI Mission was approved with an outlay of approximately ₹10,372 crore over five years. By July 2026, 15 compute providers had been empanelled, 237 projects had received support and approximately 93 lakh GPU hours had been sanctioned. The mission also reported 20 indigenous sovereign-model proposals selected for support. (Press Information Bureau)
This infrastructure can lower the entry barrier for Indian startups, research institutions and smaller technology companies that cannot independently purchase large amounts of compute.
