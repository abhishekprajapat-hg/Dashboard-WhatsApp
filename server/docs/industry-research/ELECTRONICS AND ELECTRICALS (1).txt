3. Core Pain Points & Revenue Leaks
The Electronics & Electrical manufacturing industry operates with high product complexity, short technology life cycles, global supply chains and thin manufacturing margins. Even small operational inefficiencies can significantly impact profitability.
Below are the five largest operational bottlenecks and the corresponding digitization opportunities.

1. Semiconductor & Electronic Component Supply Chain Disruptions
Unlike many manufacturing industries, electronics manufacturing depends on thousands of individual electronic components sourced globally.
A single missing semiconductor can stop production of an entire product line.
Typical vulnerable components include:
Microcontrollers
Memory ICs
Processors
MOSFETs
Power modules
LCD panels
Sensors
WiFi/Bluetooth modules
Connectors
Lithium batteries
Global events such as pandemics, geopolitical conflicts and logistics disruptions have demonstrated how fragile these supply chains can become.
Revenue Leaks
Production line stoppages
Emergency component purchases
Air freight premiums
Excess inventory of other components
Missed customer delivery dates
Lost dealer confidence
Production rescheduling
Idle labor
Lower factory utilization
