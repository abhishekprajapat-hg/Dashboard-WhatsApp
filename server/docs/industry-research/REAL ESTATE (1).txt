REAL ESTATE

1. End-to-End Business Flow
For this audit, I’m treating Real Estate primarily as a property development business covering residential and commercial projects, with sales, leasing, channel partners, construction, collections, and post-handover operations included.
Real Estate Business Process Map

The residential market entered 2026 in a more mature phase, with overall sales volumes relatively stable but transaction values remaining high as premium and luxury housing gain share. (CBRE India)

2. Digitization & Automation Mapping
Real estate is unusually suitable for digitisation because the traditional model contains multiple disconnected teams: land, construction, procurement, finance, marketing, sales and customer service.
Recommended Real Estate Technology Stack
For a mid-sized developer, a strong architecture would look like:
Lead Sources → CRM → Sales Automation → Booking Platform → ERP → Construction Management → Customer Portal → Facility Management → BI Analytics
Example software categories:
CRM: Salesforce, Zoho CRM, LeadSquared, Sell.Do, HubSpot
ERP: SAP S/4HANA, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Farvision, SAP Business One
Construction: Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Primavera P6
BIM: Autodesk Revit / Navisworks
Analytics: Power BI / Tableau
Automation: Power Automate / UiPath / Zapier / Make
Customer engagement: WhatsApp Business API + AI chatbot
Document management: SharePoint / DocuSign / digital document repositories
Facility management: CAFM/IWMS platforms
AI layer: predictive pricing, lead scoring, cash-flow forecasting, document analysis and construction-risk prediction.
This movement toward data-based decision-making is already visible in the market. CBRE, for example, launched a real-time investment-risk monitoring platform in India capable of tracking financial exposure, returns, surplus and IRR at deal and asset levels. AI is also increasingly being applied to leasing decisions using real-time utilisation, mobility and economic data rather than relying primarily on historical benchmarks. (CBRE India)
Highest-ROI Automation Opportunities
For a real estate company that is still heavily dependent on Excel, WhatsApp and manual coordination, I would prioritise:
CRM + WhatsApp Automation → Inventory System → ERP → Collection Automation → Construction Tracking → BI Dashboard → AI
Why this order?
Because the fastest ROI generally comes from preventing:
lost leads + slow follow-ups + incorrect inventory + missed collections + uncontrolled project costs.
