MEDIA AND COMMUNICATION

The following is an India-focused industry audit covering television, print, newspapers, magazines, radio, film, OTT streaming, digital publishing, advertising, public relations, social media, influencer marketing, podcasting, music, animation, VFX, gaming, live entertainment, corporate communication, content production and media technology.
1. End-to-End Business Flow
Media and communication businesses create commercial value through:
Audience attention
Information
Entertainment
storytelling
advertising access
intellectual property
brand influence
subscriptions
content licensing
events
communication strategy
creative production
The sector operates through several connected business models:
News and publishing: Story identification → research → editorial production → publishing → audience distribution → advertising or subscription revenue → engagement and retention
Film and television: Concept → development → financing → pre-production → production → post-production → distribution → monetisation → licensing
Digital content and OTT: Content strategy → creation or acquisition → platform publishing → audience acquisition → advertising or subscription → analytics → retention
Advertising agency: Client acquisition → campaign brief → strategy → creative development → media planning → campaign execution → measurement → account renewal
Public relations: Reputation assessment → communication strategy → content and media outreach → issue management → reporting → ongoing retainer
Creator and influencer business: Audience development → content production → platform distribution → brand collaboration → monetisation → community retention
Live entertainment: Event concept → artist or speaker contracting → venue and sponsor sales → ticketing → production → event delivery → settlement

1. Market, audience and cultural-trend analysis
Analyse audience demographics, language, geography, interests and media-consumption habits.
Review television, streaming, cinema, print, radio, podcast, gaming and social-media usage.
Track regional, national and international cultural trends.
Assess advertiser demand by industry and customer segment.
Study competing publications, channels, studios, agencies, creators and platforms.
Identify gaps in content, format, language, genre or distribution.
Monitor device usage, connectivity, subscription behaviour and willingness to pay.
Estimate total addressable audience and likely monetisation potential.
India’s media and entertainment industry grew to approximately ₹2.78 trillion in 2025, representing around 9% annual growth. Digital media crossed ₹1 trillion and became the sector’s largest segment, while digital advertising reached approximately ₹947 billion. (Press Information Bureau)
