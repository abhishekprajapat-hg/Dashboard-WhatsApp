ENTERTAINMENT & OTHERS

The following is an India-focused industry audit for the broad Entertainment & Other Consumer Services category. It covers amusement and theme parks, family entertainment centres, gaming and esports, sports and recreation businesses, fitness centres, wellness and beauty services, live attractions, cultural experiences, ticketed events, hobby businesses, children’s activity centres and other experience-based consumer services.
This audit separates the category from traditional media production and hospitality, while recognising that some segments overlap with film, gaming, tourism, events and digital content.

Leisure experiences
participation and social interaction

The sector operates through several connected business models:
Amusement and theme parks: Market assessment → concept and attraction development → ticket distribution → visitor arrival → attraction operations → food and merchandise → exit → repeat visitation
Family entertainment centres: Location selection → game and activity setup → local customer acquisition → admission or play-credit sale → experience delivery → birthday and group sales → membership retention
Gaming and esports: Game or competition development → player acquisition → platform access → gameplay → in-game or sponsorship monetisation → community management → retention
Sports academies and clubs: Customer acquisition → skill or fitness assessment → enrolment → coaching → competition and performance tracking → renewal
Fitness and wellness: Lead generation → consultation → membership or treatment sale → service scheduling → service delivery → progress monitoring → repeat booking
Live and cultural entertainment: Event concept → artist or venue contracting → sponsorship and ticketing → production → event delivery → settlement → audience retention
