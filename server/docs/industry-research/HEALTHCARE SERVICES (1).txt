HEALTHCARE SERVICES

The following is an India-focused industry audit covering hospitals, day-care centres, specialty clinics, primary-care practices, diagnostic laboratories, imaging centres, pharmacies, telemedicine, home healthcare, rehabilitation, mental-health services, emergency care, preventive health, health-insurance coordination and healthcare-support services.
1. End-to-End Business Flow
Healthcare services operate through several connected business models:
Hospital care: Patient acquisition or referral → registration → consultation → diagnosis → admission or treatment → discharge → billing → follow-up
Outpatient clinic: Appointment → registration → consultation → prescription or investigation → payment → follow-up and retention
Diagnostic services: Test booking → sample or image collection → analysis → medical validation → report delivery → billing → repeat testing
Telemedicine: Digital registration → appointment → remote consultation → e-prescription → payment → digital follow-up
Home healthcare: Patient assessment → care-plan design → caregiver allocation → home service delivery → monitoring → billing → care-plan renewal
Health-insurance-supported care: Eligibility verification → pre-authorisation → treatment → documentation → claim submission → insurer query resolution → settlement
