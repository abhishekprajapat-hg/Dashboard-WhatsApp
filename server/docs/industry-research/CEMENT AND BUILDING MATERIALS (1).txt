CEMENT AND BUILDING MATERIALS

The following is an India-focused industry audit covering cement manufacturing, clinker production, ready-mix concrete, aggregates, precast concrete, tiles, ceramics, sanitaryware, glass, gypsum products and construction chemicals. Your source document classifies Cement & Building Materials under manufacturing and specifically includes cement production, tiles and ceramics, and glass manufacturing.
1. End-to-End Business Flow
The industry operates through several connected value chains:
Cement manufacturing: Limestone extraction → crushing → raw-meal preparation → clinker production → cement grinding → packing → dealer or project distribution
Ready-mix concrete: Customer specification → mix design → cement and aggregate sourcing → batching → transit mixing → site delivery → testing and billing
Tiles and ceramics: Clay and mineral sourcing → body preparation → forming → drying → glazing → kiln firing → grading → distribution
Glass manufacturing: Silica and raw-material sourcing → batch preparation → melting → forming → annealing → coating or processing → inspection → dispatch
Precast and construction products: Project requirement → engineering design → mould and reinforcement preparation → casting → curing → testing → site installation

Crush limestone into smaller, consistent particles.

16. Raw-meal proportioning
Determine the correct proportion of limestone and corrective materials.

Feed materials through calibrated weigh feeders.
Maintain the required lime saturation, silica and alumina ratios.
Adjust the mix as quarry chemistry changes.
17. Raw grinding
Grind proportioned materials into fine raw meal.
Dry the material using hot process gases where applicable.
Separate fine and coarse particles.
Return oversized material for additional grinding.
Monitor mill vibration, temperature, power use and output.
Store acceptable raw meal in homogenisation silos.
18. Raw-meal homogenisation
Blend the raw meal to achieve consistent chemical composition.
Use controlled aeration and silo extraction.
Test samples regularly.
Correct abnormal variation.
Release homogenised meal to the kiln system.

20. Preheating and precalcination
Feed raw meal through cyclone preheaters.
Use hot kiln gases to heat the material.
Begin calcination before the rotary kiln.
Control gas flow, temperature and pressure.
Optimise fuel use and reduce kiln load.
21. Clinker production
Feed calcined raw material into the rotary kiln.
Heat it to clinker-forming temperatures.
Form clinker minerals that provide cement strength.
Control flame, kiln speed, feed rate and oxygen.
Monitor clinker quality and kiln stability.
Discharge hot clinker into the cooler.
22. Clinker cooling and heat recovery
Cool clinker rapidly.
Recover hot air for kiln combustion.
Control cooler efficiency and clinker temperature.
Inspect clinker size and quality.
Transport cooled clinker to storage.
23. Clinker storage
Store clinker in silos, sheds or stockpiles.
Track quantity and production date.
Prevent excessive dust and moisture exposure.
Allocate clinker to grinding units.
Transfer clinker between integrated and standalone grinding plants where required.
24. Cement grinding
Mix clinker with gypsum and selected supplementary materials.
Grind the mixture to the required fineness.
Produce ordinary Portland cement, Portland pozzolana cement, Portland slag cement or specialised grades.
Control separator settings, temperature and residue.
Test cement quality during grinding.
Store finished cement in product-specific silos.
25. Cement blending and quality adjustment
Blend cement from different mill runs where permitted.
Adjust fineness or additive proportions within approved specifications.
Verify consistency across silo contents.
Prevent cross-contamination between grades.
Release conforming cement for packing or bulk loading.

5. Future Growth Prospects
Revenue trends
India remains the world’s second-largest cement producer. Cement production reached approximately 453 million tonnes in FY2024–25, supported by housing, infrastructure and wider construction demand. (Press Information Bureau)
Official core-sector data indicates that cement production increased by 8.6% during FY2025–26 compared with the previous financial year. Production continued growing in the opening months of FY2026–27, rising 9.4% year-on-year in April 2026 and 8.4% in May 2026. (Press Information Bureau)
Industry estimates place installed cement capacity at roughly 655–668 million tonnes per annum around FY2025–26, with significant additional capacity expected through FY2028. These estimates suggest continued investment, but actual utilisation and profitability will vary by region and demand conditions. (India Brand Equity Foundation)
