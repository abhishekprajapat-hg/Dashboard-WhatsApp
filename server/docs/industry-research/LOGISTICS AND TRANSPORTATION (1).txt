LOGISTICS AND TRANSPORTATION

The following is an India-focused industry audit covering road freight, trucking, rail logistics, air cargo, shipping, ports, courier and express delivery, warehousing, third-party logistics, cold-chain services, e-commerce fulfilment, last-mile delivery, freight forwarding and multimodal transportation.
1. End-to-End Business Flow
The logistics and transportation industry connects manufacturers, farmers, importers, exporters, retailers and consumers by moving goods, information and documents across locations.
Its commercial value comes from:
Freight movement
Inventory storage
order fulfilment
shipment visibility
route optimisation
consolidation
customs support
cold-chain protection
delivery reliability
risk management
reverse logistics
The industry operates through several connected business models:
Road freight: Customer enquiry → freight quotation → vehicle allocation → pickup → line-haul transport → delivery → proof of delivery → billing and collection
Third-party logistics: Client onboarding → warehouse and transport design → inventory receipt → storage → order fulfilment → distribution → reporting → contract renewal
Courier and express delivery: Shipment booking → first-mile pickup → sorting → hub movement → destination sorting → last-mile delivery → settlement
Freight forwarding: Export or import enquiry → route and carrier selection → booking → documentation → customs clearance → international transport → destination delivery
Cold-chain logistics: Product assessment → temperature-controlled pickup → storage → monitored transportation → delivery validation → excursion management
Rail and multimodal logistics: Cargo aggregation → terminal handling → rail movement → destination terminal → road delivery → reconciliation
