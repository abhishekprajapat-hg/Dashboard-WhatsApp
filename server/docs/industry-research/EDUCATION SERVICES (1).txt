EDUCATION SERVICES

The following is an India-focused industry audit covering schools, colleges, universities, vocational education, coaching institutes, test preparation, corporate training, online learning, edtech platforms, tutoring, certification, education consulting, student mobility and placement services.
1. End-to-End Business Flow
Education services create value by developing knowledge, skills, qualifications, employability and personal capability. Unlike manufacturing, the core product is not a physical item. It is a structured learning outcome supported by teachers, curriculum, technology, assessment, student services and institutional credibility.
The sector operates through several connected business models:
School education: Market and community assessment → admissions → student onboarding → teaching → assessment → progression → parent engagement → re-enrolment
Higher education: Programme design → regulatory approval → student recruitment → admission → academic delivery → examination → degree completion → placement and alumni engagement
Coaching and test preparation: Student acquisition → diagnostic assessment → course enrolment → classroom or digital instruction → mock testing → examination support → result tracking → referral and repeat enrolment
Vocational education: Employer skill-gap analysis → programme design → learner enrolment → practical training → assessment → certification → apprenticeship or placement
Edtech and online learning: Content development → digital marketing → enrolment → platform onboarding → online delivery → engagement → assessment → certification → renewal or upselling
Corporate learning: Employer requirement → competency assessment → proposal → programme development → training delivery → evaluation → performance follow-up → annual contract renewal
